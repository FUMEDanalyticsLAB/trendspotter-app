Your sleep plan

Steady bedtime

Lights out at 23:05, within 30 minutes, every night for 14 days. A consistent bedtime is one of the simplest ways to improve sleep.

How to do it
1

At 22:35, start wind-down: dim the lights and put your phone on charge outside the bedroom.

2

Keep the same lights-out time on weekends. Consistency matters more than an early night.

3

Not sleepy yet? Read something calm in low light until you are, then switch off.

About and safety

TrendSpotter is a physician-designed wellness proof of concept. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition.
