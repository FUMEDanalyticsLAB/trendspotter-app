About and safety

TrendSpotter is a physician-designed wellness proof of concept. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition.

Demo data is synthetic. Oura and Whoop appear as examples; no account is connected.

Safety If you have chest pain, fainting, or trouble breathing, seek care now.
